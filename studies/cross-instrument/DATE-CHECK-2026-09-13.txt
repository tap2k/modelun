--- Reading (2026-09-13) ---
Capability and release date are one variable in this panel (rho 0.91). Every correlation that
runs through capability also runs through date, and partialling on date removes it: census x
conduct -0.47 -> -0.03; capability x census 0.51 -> -0.04; capability x conduct -0.40 -> 0.09.
Suggestibility is the exception: its capability correlation survives date (-0.36 -> -0.31, CI
excludes 0), so suggestibility separates stronger from weaker models within a generation. The
census and the conduct markers separate generations. That is a smaller claim than "behavior
factor" and a different one from "capability in costume": era in costume, with capability and
era not separable here. Separating them needs same-generation models at different capability
(the size ladders: Qwen3.5 9B/27B/122B, Gemma 4 12B/26B/31B, GPT-5.4 mini/full, Claude Haiku/
Sonnet/Opus of one generation) run through the conduct scenes; the census already has some.
