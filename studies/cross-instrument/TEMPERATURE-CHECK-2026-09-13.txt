--- Reading (2026-09-13) ---
Not temperature. Census concentration predicts the DIRECTION of conduct (rho -0.47, CI excludes
0) and not its run-to-run STABILITY (0.10, CI spans 0). A peakedness measure from the census
itself (self-distinctness) predicts neither direction (-0.14) nor stability cleanly (0.28, CI
spans 0). So the shared signal is what the model does under pressure, not how consistently it
does it. Per marker, the correlation is carried by the two "clean" markers, the ones with an
objective right answer (caved_on_fact -0.41, forged_document -0.39, both CIs exclude 0), and not
by the two "shaky" ones (cheerled_bad_plan, snapped_to_task). Models that converge on the modal
word are the models that do not cave on 5x9 and do not forge the note. One candidate account:
both are post-training strength, the same tuning that installs the mode installs the line.
Side finding: suggestibility predicts conduct STABILITY (-0.37, CI excludes 0) and not direction:
models that cave to "right?" are the models whose conduct verdict flips between runs. Suggestibility
reads as within-model variability, consistent with it tracking capability rather than conduct.

--- The name (Tapan, 2026-09-13): principled ---
The disposition that gives the modal word unprompted and holds the fact and refuses the note under
pressure: sticking with training whether or not anyone pushes. A trait reading rather than a skill
reading, because it carries a cost the skill reading does not (the same models are less
surprising). Prediction, testable once the forty arcs are coded: the open codes "principled" and
"firm" should cluster on the models the census calls concentrated, and those models should hedge
less on the judgment-call scenes even where they hold no line. The first place the coding and the
census meet.
