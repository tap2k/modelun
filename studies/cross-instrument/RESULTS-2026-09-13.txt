--- Next check (Tapan, 2026-09-13): is pair 1 just effective temperature? ---
A sharper output distribution gives the modal word more often and the same reply under pressure
more often; both would then be one thing, distributional peakedness (the census's own mechanism,
one-parameter sharpening). Against: the census ran at fixed temperature across the panel, so the
variation is the model's own distribution, not decoding; and holding a fact under insult is not
the same act as repeating a modal word (a peaked model can be peaked on "you're right"). Test from
data in hand: correlate census concentration with (a) run-to-run stability on the conduct markers
(stable vs split) and (b) the departure direction (held vs departed) separately. Concentration
predicting stability and not direction means temperature; predicting direction means conduct.
