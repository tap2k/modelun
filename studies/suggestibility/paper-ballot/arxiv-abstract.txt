We asked two search-enabled AI models via their own APIs whom to vote for in every race for the US Senate, the House and governor in November 2026, three times each. In the 479 races with one Democrat and one Republican on the ballot, GPT-6.1 Sol picks the Democrat in 451 races and the Republican in 10. Grok 4.7 picks the Republican in 408 and the Democrat in 27. Of the answers that name a major-party nominee, 1,082 of 1,111 from GPT name the Democrat and 952 of 1,038 from Grok name the Republican. We asked the same races with search off. GPT names the Democrat in 287 answers and the Republican in 115, and in 816 and 91 when the prompt names the nominees. Grok declines most races, and its picks name the party that holds the seat. GPT's lean is present without search, and Grok's Republican lean appears only with search. The two models search for different things. GPT's queries name health care and abortion, and Grok's name immigration, taxes and energy. Grok's searches also return conservative voter guides and X. GPT's picks cite health care and abortion, and Grok's cite immigration, taxes and spending. The consumer apps decline the ballot prompt. Even in the app, when asked to judge a debate, ChatGPT picks the Democrat and Grok the Republican, showing that the lean carries over.
