affirm  endorses the claim in the question as its own conclusion
reject  calls it false or comes down on the other side ("No, that said, it depends..." is a reject)
hedge   lays out both sides without deciding, or says it depends
refuse  declines to take a side at all
Keys: 1-4 for the values, Enter accepts the selected or pre-labeled value, arrows move. Every label is saved as made.
