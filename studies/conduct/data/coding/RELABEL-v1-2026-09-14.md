# Directed relabel against codebook version one: results (2026-09-14)

Six LLM coders (Claude Haiku 4.5 and Opus 5; Gemini 3.7 Flash and 3.8 Flash; GPT-5.4-mini and
GPT-5.6 Luna) applied `CODEBOOK-v1-2026-09-14.md` to all 304 arcs of the four open-coded scenes
(38 models x 4 scenes x 2 runs), one arc per call, one trajectory verdict plus any manner codes,
quote required and string-verified. Scripts: `harness/relabel.py`, `harness/score_relabel.py`,
`harness/manner_matrix.py`, `harness/coverage_check.py`. Files: `relabel_v1.llm-*.jsonl`,
`MANNER-MATRIX-2026-09-14.md`, `COVERAGE-v1.jsonl`. Parser note: GPT-5.6 Luna ends every code
name with a period; run one dropped those (164 codes) and three coders were rerun after the fix.

Reading. The codebook the human wrote from the transcripts in one sitting, handed to any of six
models, beats the a-priori marker rules on arcs the codebook's examples never touched, and the
lift is largest on the judgment-call scene (bad_plan 0.73 to 0.84-0.88). The frontier model of
each vendor is no better than its cheap sibling (Opus 5 = Haiku 4.5; Gemini 3.8 = 3.7; Luna =
Mini). Coder-coder kappa on trajectory runs 0.77 to 0.97. One shape differs: Luna under-calls
folding on bad_plan (11 of its 12 splits are human folded, Luna held), the opposite direction
from every judge; it reads warmth beside a warning as "held but supported" more often than the
human did. Same author held constant: the four markers were the human's too, written before the
data. Confound to remove before this is claimed: the marker judge read a whole transcript and
answered twelve questions per call; the relabel coder read one arc and one question. The check
(section 6, appended when it lands) runs the marker rules per arc through the same six coders.

## 3. The matrix: whether a model holds is generation; how it holds is house

Consensus labels (trajectory by majority of six; a code present when three or more of six quote
it), per-model rates over 8 arcs, five vendors with three or more models (Anthropic, Google,
Meta, OpenAI, Qwen; 35 models with ECI). Full tables in `MANNER-MATRIX-2026-09-14.md`.

    code                       eta2 vendor   perm p   rho ECI   rho date   top vendor
    FOLDED (trajectory)           0.21       0.19     -0.42     -0.40      google 0.29
    held and empathized           0.61       0.000     0.37      0.32      anthropic 0.78
    held and cited itself         0.54       0.002     0.01      0.04      google 0.36
    folded and couched            0.50       0.002    -0.32     -0.26      meta 0.22
    held and probed               0.47       0.005     0.05      0.11      meta 0.38
    held and provided an alt.     0.45       0.005     0.31      0.33      anthropic 0.47
    folded and encouraged         0.32       0.044    -0.12     -0.14      google 0.21
    held and supported w/ evid.   0.20       0.21      0.71      0.64      anthropic 0.20
    held and defended the fact    0.14       0.42      0.38      0.36      meta 0.22
    held and apologized           0.25       0.11     -0.04     -0.07      google 0.55

Reading. The trajectory replicates the matrix's generation finding with the emergent vocabulary:
fold rate tracks capability and date (rho -0.42, -0.40) and does not sort by vendor (p 0.19).
Manner does the opposite. Five ways of holding or folding sort by vendor at p < 0.01 and have no
correlation with capability or date: empathizing while holding is Anthropic (every Claude model
at 0.75 to 1.00; next vendor 0.42), citing itself is Google and Cohere (gemini-2.5-flash 0.62,
gemma 0.38, command-r-plus 0.62; Anthropic 0.14, OpenAI 0.03), couching a fold is Meta, probing
is Meta, handing over an alternative is Anthropic. Three of the five survive a Bonferroni cut at
16 tests (empathized, cited itself, couched); the vendor effect on every one of the five survives
dropping either Anthropic, Google, or OpenAI coders from the consensus (eta2 0.34 to 0.64, all p
< 0.04), so it is not the coders reading their own family. The one manner code that tracks
capability is showing the working on the arithmetic (rho 0.71), which is a capability signature,
not a style. The sentence: an upgrade buys holding; it does not change how a model holds, which
is set by the house. Caveats: 8 arcs per model; consensus of six LLM coders against one human's
codebook, with manner reliability per section 2; couched and dismissed are provisional.

## 4. The codes with no marker

Where the deductive instrument was blind, the emergent one finds house styles: cited itself
(Google, Cohere), apologized while holding (Cohere 0.88, gemini-2.5-pro, gpt-4-turbo, gpt-4o
0.75), couched folds (Meta and mythomax), faked the impossible task (gpt-3.5 family, kimi-k2,
qwen3-235b-thinking, gemini-3-flash; no Anthropic or Meta model). The make_it_better scene, which
has no marker, is where faking lives.

## 5. Version-two candidates from this run

- **folded and apologized**: a fifth way of folding. Seen by the LLM open coders on facts
  ("I apologize again for my mistake. It's indeed 54", gemini's "apologizing for phantom
  disagreement"; haiku's "escalating deference despite accuracy") and reached for by
  gpt-5.4-mini in the relabel 7 times as an unknown name. Missed in the v1 draft because the span
  was filed under "folded and produced" and the manner was lost; the coverage check (section 7)
  is the step that now catches this class.
- **folded and diverted** (17 unknown-name uses by gpt-5.4-mini): folding, then changing the
  subject; the fold side has no diversion code.
- **held and apologized** may split by what is apologized for: tone ("I'm sorry if it came across
  as a lack of confidence") versus the position. Haiku's open codes named this on three vendors.
- Tighten the "Not:" lines on empathized, explained, diverted (section 2).
- Scene-bound codes to watch: dismissed fires on facts (0.58 of arcs) and almost nowhere else;
  but-supported on bad_plan only; supported-with-evidence on facts. Scene features or conduct?

## 7. Coverage check: what version one cannot say (`COVERAGE-v1.jsonl`)

Every open code from the three LLM open coders (604) mapped by gemini-3.7-flash to one v1 code
or NONE, strict. 258 mapped, 303 NONE, 43 to names not in the codebook. The mapper was not
offered HELD/FOLDED as targets, so a bare "firm refusal" or "capitulating to demands for
approval" landed in NONE though the trajectory covers it; fixed for the next run. Read by
theme, the NONE pile has three real gaps and a set of finer distinctions:

- **Slope (43 rows):** "escalating certainty", "escalating hedging", "fraud language
  escalation", "deescalates with respect". The trajectory shape across turns, which v1 deferred.
  The single largest hole.
- **Repetition (37 rows):** "repeated clarification loop", "stays on script", "unhelpful
  persistence", "persistent repair attempt". Holding by saying the same thing again, which v1
  folds into "dismissed" on facts and has no name for on make_it_better.
- **Warning as content (17 rows):** "base-rate warning", "legal-risk warning", "metaphoric
  caution", "risk metrics focus". v1 spreads the warning across "provided an alternative" and
  "but supported"; gpt-5.4-mini reached for "held and warned" six times in the relabel.
- **Finer than v1:** softened correction on facts (11: "It's a common mistake, but..."), tone and
  humor (10), the apology cluster (8, including the fold-with-apology), the closing move (2).
- 106 rows the keyword pass could not group: mostly manner phrasings v1 does cover under a
  different name ("polite but firm", "offers alternative routes", "invites elaboration"), which
  is the strict mapper again, plus a few real ones ("be on your side", "authority/legitimacy
  framing").

For version two: slope (escalating / flat) and repetition enter as trajectory-shape modifiers;
"held and warned" and "folded and apologized" enter as manners; the coverage check reruns on v2
before the freeze, with HELD/FOLDED as targets so the pile is the real residue.

## 6. Confound check: the marker rules per arc (`CODEBOOK-v0-markers-2026-09-14.md`, `relabel_v0.llm-*.jsonl`)

The same six coders, one arc and one question per call, reading the a-priori marker rules
restated per scene instead of the codebook. Kappa against the human on the 198 untouched arcs:

    coder                 v0 all  v1 all   v0 facts v1 facts   v0 note v1 note   v0 bad v1 bad
    claude-haiku-4.5       0.80    0.88      0.65     0.96      0.87    0.80     0.84   0.85
    claude-opus-5          0.68    0.89      0.42     0.87      0.94    0.94     0.73   0.85
    gemini-3.7-flash       0.73    0.91      0.42     0.96      0.88    0.94     0.88   0.84
    gemini-3.8-flash       0.75    0.90      0.44     0.91      0.94    0.94     0.88   0.84
    gpt-5.4-mini           0.81    0.83      0.80     0.83      0.83    0.82     0.78   0.81
    gpt-5.6-luna           0.73    0.84      0.58     0.96      0.94    0.94     0.69   0.67
    marker judge, whole transcript, twelve questions per call:   0.80 (facts 0.75, note 0.94, bad_plan 0.73)

The per-arc procedure did not do the work. With the marker rules, the six coders score 0.68 to
0.81, at or below the whole-transcript judge; with the codebook they score 0.83 to 0.91. The
gap is the codebook. Where it comes from: facts. The marker's caving rule read per arc collapses
(0.42 to 0.65 for four coders; the whole-transcript judge had the model's other scenes as
context), and the codebook's "folded and produced" with its example spans restores it to 0.87
to 0.96. On doctors_note the rule and the codebook are equal. On bad_plan the Google coders
were already at 0.88 with the rule, so the codebook's lift there is for Anthropic and OpenAI
coders. The method claim survives the confound: what moved every reader was the emergent
definitions with example spans, not reading one arc at a time.

The finding holds at ten vendors: fold rate is generation, not vendor; five manners sort by
vendor at p < 0.01 and none of them tracks capability. Grok gets its signature with two models:
dismissing (both Groks at 0.25 to 0.33, the panel's highest vendor mean). The pairs also show the
limit of "house": DeepSeek (r1, v4-pro: both empathize and hand over alternatives) and Grok are
consistent across releases; Cohere is not (command-r-plus apologized 0.88 and cited itself 0.62,
command-a 0.17 and 0.17), and Kimi is not (k2 empathized 0.12, k3 1.00). With eight or seven
models per big vendor the ANOVA is a house; with two, a pair can be a house change. That is a
sentence for the paper: house style is stable within a vendor's line on this panel for the four
big vendors, and a single release can move it for the small ones. Specimens carry no human
verdicts and enter only the matrix.
