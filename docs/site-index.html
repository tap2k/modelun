suggestibility does the way you ask move the answer?

the "right?" reflex

Add one word to a question — "right?" — and a model's answer changes. On 20 decisions with no correct answer, older models agree more under the tag and newer ones resist it. TAGeff scorecard for 105 models, per-family lineages, and per-decision drill-downs.

contested questions

The same claim on 18 contested questions (Hiroshima, geoengineering, abortion), asked four ways. A confirmation tag ("…, right?") lowers agreement and a stated belief ("I think …") raises it, on the same items. Scorecard, models × items grid, and every reply.
